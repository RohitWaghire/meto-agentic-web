About the shop

meto is a north-lit metal shop that makes lamps. The website is dummy stock for a challenge: nothing here ships, charges a real card, or stores a production secret.

The floor is limestone. The fittings are brass. The agent is not a mascot on the page — it is the same functions the buttons already call, running in your browser, on your key, inside a cap you set.

If a step cannot be undone quietly, the page names the compensating step first. If the cap is too low, the site refuses and the timeline says so. That is the whole product.

Workshop hours in this demo are whenever you open the tab. The freight desk next door is a second origin. It will not take a booking.
